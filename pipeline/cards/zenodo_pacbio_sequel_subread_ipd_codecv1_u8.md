# PacBio Sequel Subread Polymerase Kinetics: Per-Base Inter-Pulse Duration (IPD, CodecV1 8-bit) from the CC BY 4.0 Veillonella parvula SMRT Cell (Zenodo 13306684)

- Candidate id: `zenodo_pacbio_sequel_subread_ipd_codecv1_u8`
- Width: uint8
- Quantity: Per-base inter-pulse duration (IPD) of the DNA polymerase, i.e. the time in camera frames (80 fps) between successive fluorescent incorporation pulses. The Sequel instrument stores it by default as an 8-bit CodecV1 code: 0-63 are exact frames, 64-127 are 2-frame steps, 128-191 are 4-frame steps, 192-255 are 8-frame steps, capped at 952 frames. Primary field is the BAM aux tag 'ip' (B:C uint8 array, one value per called base). Pulse width ('pw') is excluded, so the recipe stays one quantity.
