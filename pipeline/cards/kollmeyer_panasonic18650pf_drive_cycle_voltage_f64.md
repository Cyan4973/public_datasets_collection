- Quantity: Measured cell terminal voltage (V; sense leads welded to the terminals), logged every 0.1 s by a Digatron Universal Battery Tester. The cell is driven with drive-cycle power profiles for an electric Ford F150 pack scaled to one cell (US06, HWFET, UDDS, LA92, Neural-Network, Cycle 1-4) at ambient -20, -10, 0, 10 and 25 °C, plus the temperature-rise runs.
