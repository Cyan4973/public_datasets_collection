Validate each header: byte0 = year-1900 (124), month, day, h, m, s, site = 5, nbands = 2. Band descriptors are big-endian '>HHHBB' = (25, 75, 401, ref level, atten) and (75, 180, 401, ...). Emit bytes 24..825 of each record as one uint8 [T, 802] array per day; alternatively split per band. Reject short outage days (e.g. under 5,000 records). Suggested subset: the 1st, 11th and 21st of each month of 2024, 36 days in total.

RSTN is a different, unambiguously public-domain source (USAF/NOAA) for the same solar radio dynamic-spectrum material. Nothing downstream matches. The related accepted csiro_parkes_uwl_search_mode_u8 is a pulsar-search filterbank at L-band, a different instrument and signal.
- Homogeneity: One station (Sagamore Hill), one spectrograph and analyzer configuration (band A 25-75 MHz and band B 75-180 MHz, 401 channels each, ref level 20, atten 0 in probes), one year (2024, solar maximum) and one 3-second cadence. Other stations (San Vito, Palehua, Learmonth, Holloman) are excluded to avoid receiver-to-receiver scale differences. Bands A and B have different baselines (about 38 vs 58 DN); the builder may emit them as separate per-band samples.
- Risks: (1) A judge may see a second radio dynamic-spectrum family (Parkes filterbank is accepted), although the source, band and physics differ.
