# OSIRIS-REx Laser Altimeter (OLA) Level-2 Bennu Lidar Point Coordinates XYZ Float64

- Candidate id: `orex_ola_l2_lidar_point_xyz_f64`
- Width: float64
- Quantity: Bennu body-fixed Cartesian positions (x, y, z in metres) of individual OLA laser returns. These are the native IEEE754LSBDouble fields x/y/z of the PDS4 L2 science table (scil2id*.dat, 186-byte records), emitted interleaved as an N×3 array per file. Range, az/el, intensities, lat/lon/radius, spacecraft position, time strings and flags are not primary.

- Natural record: One complete published OLA L2 scan product (one scil2id .dat file with its PDS4 label), with all of its lidar returns emitted as an N×3 float64 xyz array. Proposed deterministic scope: every data_calibrated_v2 L2 file under 200 MB in the preliminary_survey (8 files, 85–102 MB, ~516k records each), recon_b (42 files of ~17 MB, ~98k records each), recon_c (2) and sample_collection (3 small) phases. That gives about 55 files and about 9M returns. The 594 MB Orbit-B files (911) and the 1.4–9.1 GB recon scans are excluded by the size bound.

- Decode path: curl each .dat and .xml from the PSI SBN HTTPS archive (supports Range; the label's file_size and records allow pinning). Parse the PDS4 label with xml.etree to confirm record_length=186, 23 fields and x/y/z at field_location 115/123/131 (IEEE754LSBDouble) plus flag_status at 73 (SignedLSB2). Iterate records with struct '<3d' at byte offset 114 and write little-endian float64 interleaved xyz. Missing-value policy: keep only returns with flag_status in {0,1,100,101}, since 2/3/102/103 mean no return or missing sample. Alternatively keep all records and document that choice.
- Novelty kind: new_quantity
- Novelty evidence: novelty.py --url https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds4/orex/orex.ola/ --terms osiris-rex ola bennu altimeter: no URL, registry, ledger or downstream match. The only 'altimeter' hit is nasa_pds_mola_megdr_i16, a gridded Mars topography raster rather than per-return point geometry. No lidar point coordinates exist at 64 bits locally or downstream. dc_lidar_2015 provides only GPS time (f64), intensity and class, and goose VLS-128 xyz is f32 from a car-mounted scanner.
- Homogeneity: One instrument (OLA, mostly the low/high-energy laser per laser_selection), one frame (Bennu body-fixed) and one unit (m). The values are SPICE-computed full doubles: 0 of 300 probed xyz values are float32-exact. Mission phases differ in altitude and scan pattern (preliminary survey ~7 km vs recon ~0.6–1 km). That is geometric variation of the same quantity, not a different unit or scale lattice. The builder may restrict to fewer phases if a judge objects.
- Risks: Poor extraction ratio: 24 of 186 bytes are kept, so about 1.7 GB is downloaded for about 217 MB. The absolute kept signal is large and no leaner xyz product exists, since data_reduced is L1 instrument-frame. The PSI host ran at only about 0.4 MB/s single-stream, so 1.7 GB takes about 1.2 h, within the 6 h limit, and resumable curl is mandatory. The size-bounded file selection must be stated explicitly as the scope rule. Flag filtering changes the per-file value count and must be documented. The superseded data_calibrated (v1) and l2a_v20/v21 Orbit-B-only collections must not be mixed in.
- Probe evidence: The directory listings of data_calibrated_v2 phases are live, with sizes: preliminary_survey 8 files 85–102 MB; recon_b 63 files (42×17M, 18×1.4G, 323M, 1.0G, 9.1G); recon_c 63M and 110M; sample_collection 17M, 5.5M×2, 422M; orbit_a 3×380M; detailed_survey 19×202–305M; orbit_b 911×594M. The PDS4 label 20190701_ola_scil2id04000.xml lists 23 fields including x/y/z/range IEEE754LSBDouble, records=3,351,360, record_length=186. The label 20181204_ola_scil2id01000.xml gives records=516,063, file_size=95,987,718, and the flag_status semantics.
