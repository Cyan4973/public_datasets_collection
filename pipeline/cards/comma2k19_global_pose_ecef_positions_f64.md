# comma2k19 Global Pose ECEF Camera Positions Float64

- Candidate id: `comma2k19_global_pose_ecef_positions_f64`
- Width: float64
- Quantity: Per-frame ECEF (Earth-centred, Earth-fixed) position of the recording camera in metres at 20 Hz. It comes from comma.ai's tightly coupled INS/GNSS/vision pose optimizer, which uses raw GNSS processed by Laika. Stored as a row-major (N,3) x,y,z array; values are about -2.7e6, -4.3e6, 3.9e6 m with sub-mm digits, so they genuinely need float64.
