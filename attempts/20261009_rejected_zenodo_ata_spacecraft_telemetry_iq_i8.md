# zenodo_ata_spacecraft_telemetry_iq_i8

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: rejected
- Candidate dataset: Allen Telescope Array spacecraft-telemetry IQ recordings by Daniel Estévez. These are native SigMF ci8 recordings (interleaved int8 I/Q), one centred 16 MiB window from each of the 17 ci8 recordings: Orion x2 (S-band, 10.24 Msps), JUICE 1a/5c, Euclid p1/p2, Hera x/y (2.048 Msps), Psyche 0x (4.096 Msps), and ESCAPADE Blue/Gold ×2 passes ×x/y (256 ksps).
- Source: Zenodo records 7395644, 7396606, 7856893, 7856895, 8111163, 8111510, 10023378, 14060873 and 17623374 (https://zenodo.org/records/7856893). All are CC BY 4.0 in the record metadata and in each sidecar's `core:license`.
- Why it looked promising: It would have been the first 8-bit complex-baseband IQ family; local IQ families exist only at 16 bits (ci16) and 32 bits (cf32). It met the retry condition of the blocked `zenodo_sigmf_ci8` entry: a substantial, native ci8 SigMF source carrying explicit CC BY terms.
- Failure class: redundant (zlsim breadth gate WEAK)
- What happened: The driver's download fetched 285,335,246 bytes in 1965 s. Build, verify and gate.py all passed with no warnings: 17 samples, 285,212,672 values, every window SHA-256 pinned, no dead or constant stretch, clipping at most 0.025%, standard deviation 5.2 to 34.7. `tools/autocollect/zlsim.py gate` then exited 3 with verdict WEAK. The nearest family is local `nasa_pds_sharad_edr_raw_echo_i8:sharad_edr_ss19_echo_i8`, at distance 0.0278 (threshold 0.05) with compression loss 0.0 (threshold 0.03); this recipe's own compression ratio is 1.335. The next neighbours are PacBio IPD codecv1 u8 (0.0546), EM302 water-column i8 (0.0669) and Boreas Velodyne intensity u8 (0.0672). At 8 bits the material is zero-mean, near-Gaussian receiver noise with a weak carrier, the same compression regime as SHARAD raw echoes. The fallback 2.048 Msps X-band subset is even more noise-dominated (spectral peaks 1.6 to 2.1 dB), and selecting only the high-SNR recordings would be cherry-picking to beat the gate.
- Evidence: zlsim JSON at /tmp/autocollect/zenodo_ata_spacecraft_telemetry_iq_i8/zlsim.json. The full recipe is staged at staging/zenodo_ata_spacecraft_telemetry_iq_i8/ (selection.tsv, window_sha256.tsv, scripts/ata_ci8.py).
- Logs: .data/pipeline/logs/zenodo_ata_spacecraft_telemetry_iq_i8/download.20261009_015508.log; .data/logs/zenodo_ata_spacecraft_telemetry_iq_i8/{build,verify}.latest.log
- Decision: Reject as statistically and compression-equivalent to the accepted SHARAD EDR raw-echo int8 family. Do not retry with other noise-dominated 8-bit IQ sources, such as the screened-out Tampere GNSS low-quantization i8 candidate.
- Retry conditions: Retry only if the zlsim library or gate thresholds change so that this material scores OK against `nasa_pds_sharad_edr_raw_echo_i8`, or with a high-SNR 8-bit IQ source whose bytes are structured rather than noise-like. Check such a source with a quick zlsim measurement before building.
