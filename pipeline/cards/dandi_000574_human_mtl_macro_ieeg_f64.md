- Risks: (1) This is de-identified human clinical recording data. It is openly published on DANDI under CC BY 4.0 with IRB approval, and the corpus already holds human EEG (CHB-MIT), but the judge may ask for this to be stated. (2) Breadth: it is noisy, full-mantissa f64, and could sit near another noisy f64 family in zlsim. It is oscillatory with strong 1/f structure and no lattice, which should differ from monotone or smooth families, but this is unmeasured. (3) The 'volts' unit label conflicts with the microvolt magnitudes. (4) The publisher concatenated trial epochs, which is why splitting at timestamp gaps is recommended. (5) Assets are 1.4-7 GB each, so the build must use range fetches and never download whole files.
