# JWST NIRCam Short-Wave Full-Frame Uncalibrated Up-the-Ramp Exposures UInt16

- Candidate id: `mast_jwst_nircam_sw_uncal_ramps_u16`
- Width: uint16
- Quantity: Raw H2RG infrared-detector readings in DN for every pixel at every non-destructive group read of a NIRCam short-wavelength imaging exposure. Source: SCI extension of *_nrc[ab][1-4]_uncal.fits (Level1bModel), BITPIX 16, BZERO 32768, BUNIT 'DN', NAXIS 4 = 2048×2048×NGROUPS×NINTS.
- Source: https://stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/jwst/public/
- Resources: https://stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/jwst/public/jw02736/jw02736001001/jw02736001001_02101_00001_nrcb1_uncal.fits, https://stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/jwst/public/jw02736/jw02736001001/jw02736001001_02101_00001_nrca1_uncal.fits, https://stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/jwst/public/jw02738/jw02738001001/, https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use

- Natural record: One exposure's full SCI ramp cube for one short-wave detector, e.g. FULL/MEDIUM8/NGROUPS=8/NINTS=1: 2048×2048×8 = 33,554,432 uint16 values (67.1 MB) from a 75,556,800-byte uncal file. Emit the whole cube in native axis order: x fastest, then y, group, integration. Do not tile or crop; reference pixels are part of the record.

- Decode path: curl the pinned uncal FITS files (uncompressed) from stpubdata. In Python, walk the 2880-byte header blocks past the primary HDU (NAXIS=0) to EXTNAME 'SCI'. Assert BITPIX=16, BZERO=32768, BSCALE=1, NAXIS=4, NAXIS1=NAXIS2=2048 and a pinned NAXIS3/NAXIS4. Read NAXIS1*NAXIS2*NAXIS3*NAXIS4*2 bytes; the uint16 value is the big-endian int16 plus 32768, i.e. the big-endian 16-bit word XOR 0x8000 (array('H') + byteswap + xor). Write uint16 little-endian. Ignore the GROUP, INT_TIMES, ZEROFRAME and ASDF extensions (auxiliary at most). Pure stdlib only.
- Novelty kind: new_source
- Novelty evidence: novelty.py --url https://stpubdata.s3.amazonaws.com/jwst/public/ --terms jwst nircam uncal ramp found no URL, registry, ledger or downstream matches. The only hit was 'ramp' in an unrelated power-grid recipe. No JWST, STScI-bucket or infrared-ramp material exists at any width. The nearest 16-bit families are single-read raw camera frames (nasa_pds_mastcamz_raw_i16, mast_iue at 8-bit, sdss frames in staging). NIRCam uncal data differ structurally: non-destructive up-the-ramp reads, so the group axis carries accumulating signal, cosmic-ray jumps and 1/f noise.
- Homogeneity: Keep to one instrument and mode: NIRCam SW H2RG detectors NRCA1–4 and NRCB1–4, EXP_TYPE=NRC_IMAGE, SUBARRAY=FULL, and one pinned readout geometry (recommended: READPATT=MEDIUM8, NGROUPS=8, NINTS=1, giving identical 75,556,800-byte files). ERO program 2736 obs 001 has 84 such files and program 2738 obs 001 has 74. Exclude long-wave detectors (nrcalong/nrcblong), other instruments, subarrays/TSO and other NGROUPS values. Filters (F090W/F150W/F200W) change the sky signal level but not the unit, detector physics or ramp structure. Spread picks across all 8 SW detectors and both programs, about 12 exposures, ≤~0.8 GB.
- Risks: (1) Natural records are large (67 MB), so sample count is capped near 12 by the 1 GB limit, below the ~20 guideline. Alternative: NGROUPS=6 FULL exposures (50.4 MB files, e.g. programs 1181/1345) would allow ~16 records. Pin one geometry; do not mix. (2) archive.stsci.edu is proxy-blocked right now, so download.sh cannot re-fetch the policy page; rely on the IUE recipe evidence. (3) JWST is a NASA/ESA/CSA mission; the MAST policy has no JWST carve-out, but the judge may ask. (4) Saturated pixels (65535) and reference pixels are native; keep them. (5) Not a focus domain (astronomy instrumentation); proposed because the focus-domain hosts were unreachable and this is a strong, cheap-to-decode, native-uint16 family.
