# CSIRO Parkes UWL/Medusa 8-bit Search-Mode Filterbank Data (P1018 Proxima Centauri)

- Candidate id: `csiro_parkes_uwl_search_mode_u8`

- Quantity: Native 8-bit requantized detected radio power and cross-power (coherency products AA, BB, CR, CI) from the Parkes 64 m Ultra-Wideband Low receiver and Medusa backend in PSRFITS SEARCH mode.
