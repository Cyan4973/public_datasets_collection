- Decode path: curl the whole SICD .nitf. In pure Python, parse the NITF 2.1 file header (HL at bytes 354-360; NUMI and image segment lengths) and the 512-byte image subheader. Assert PVTYPE='R', ABPP/NBPP=32, IC='NC', NBANDS=2 with ISUBCAT I and Q, IMODE='P', NBPR=NBPC=1 and NUMI=1. Read the DES XML after the image segment and assert PixelType RE32F_IM32F with matching NumRows/NumCols. Then struct-unpack the image data as big-endian '>f' and write it little-endian, keeping I/Q interleaved per pixel. Byte count must equal rows*cols*8.
