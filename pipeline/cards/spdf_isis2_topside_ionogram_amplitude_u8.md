- Decode path: Pure-stdlib CDF v2.6 reader. Magic cdf2 6002 is big-endian network encoding with 4-byte offsets. Path: CDR -> GDR at 0x138 -> zVDR chain. Find the VDR named 'ampl' (DataType 11 = CDF_UINT1, zNumDims 1, dim 223, Flags=5 meaning record variance plus compression). Its CPR at the VDR's CPRorSPR offset reads (24, 11, cType=5 GZIP, rfu, pCount=1). Walk the VXR chain to its CVVR (record type 13) entries. For each, take cSize bytes and decompress with gzip/zlib (wbits=31; fall back to raw zlib), giving (maxrec+1)*223 bytes. Validate the length against MaxRec, then write raw uint8. Probed: ampl maxrec=640 -> 641x223 = 142,943 values in a 191 KB file. 'freq' (CDF_FLOAT per record), 'delay_time'/'v_height' (223 floats) and Epoch can be auxiliary series.
