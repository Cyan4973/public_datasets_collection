# USGS ShakeMap Ground-Motion Rasters Float32 — 2026-08-27

## Outcome

Accepted `usgs_shakemap_ground_motion_f32`: fourteen complete ground-motion
rasters from the official USGS ShakeMap for the 2025 M8.8 Kamchatka Peninsula
earthquake (`us6000qw60`, product version 16).

The recipe contributes 5,520,480 native float32 values and 22,081,920 primary
bytes. Every sample is one 496x795 spatial layer; the family includes paired
mean and uncertainty fields for MMI, PGA, PGV, and pseudo-spectral acceleration
at 0.3, 0.6, 1.0, and 3.0 seconds.

## New domain and shape

This is the first accepted earthquake ground-motion raster family. The
committed corpus and attempt registry contain seismic waveforms and scalar
earthquake records, but no spatial hazard grids. These fields add two-dimensional
source-distance attenuation, directional rupture structure, site effects, and
measure-specific mean/uncertainty pairings.

The fourteen layers are nonconstant and byte-distinct. Each natural sample is
kept as a complete map rather than split into artificial tiles.

## Source, rights, and representation

The source is the versioned `raster.zip` attached to the current USGS-produced
ShakeMap for event `us6000qw60`. The archive, event-detail response, and the
official USGS copyrights-and-credits page are pinned by size and SHA-256. The
rights page states that USGS-authored or produced data and information are in
the U.S. public domain; the recipe preserves USGS and event attribution.

Every selected ESRI BIL header declares `PIXELTYPE FLOAT`, `NBITS 32`, and
`BYTEORDER LSBFIRST`. The recipe therefore extracts the native IEEE-754 words
without numeric conversion. MMI remains in linear intensity units. PGA, PGV,
and PSA means remain in the source natural-log physical-unit representation,
and their uncertainty layers remain in source log-sigma units. All resulting
payloads are little-endian.

## Missing values and verification

The headers declare 999.0 as NoData, but none of the fourteen selected rasters
contains that sentinel; there are also no NaNs or infinities. Verification pins
the rights, event, product, archive, member inventory, paired headers, grid
geometry, layer order, compressed sizes, CRCs, source/output hashes, exact
value counts, extrema, distinct-value counts, and aggregate totals. It reparses
the archive and compares every generated byte, index entry, statistics record,
and output filename.
