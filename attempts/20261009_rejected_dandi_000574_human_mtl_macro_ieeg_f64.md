# dandi_000574_human_mtl_macro_ieeg_f64

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: rejected
- Candidate dataset: DANDI:000574 v0.250815.1108, human MTL working-memory dataset (Boran/Sarnthein, Zurich): macro depth-electrode intracranial EEG (acquisition/ecephys.ieeg, Neuralynx ATLAS, 2 kHz), native float64
- Source: https://dandiarchive.org/dandiset/000574/0.250815.1108 (S3 blobs range-fetched through the HDF5 chunk B-trees)
- Why it looked promising: there is no intracranial EEG and no continuous neural field-potential family at 64 bits in the local corpus. The license is CC-BY-4.0 with OpenAccess and ethics approval (KEK Zürich PB-2016-02055, per doi:10.1038/s41597-020-0364-3). The values are native float64 with no lattice. The publisher's concatenated trial epochs split cleanly at timestamp gaps that match intervals/trials exactly (442 trials × 16,000 samples).
- Failure class: byte-level breadth (zlsim WEAK, redundant)
- What happened: the recipe was authored, downloaded by the driver (510,261,512 bytes, 661 s), built and verified cleanly, and passed gate.py. Output: 3,536 samples (ses-01 of 9 subjects × 8 evenly spaced channels × every trial epoch), 56,576,000 values, 452,608,000 bytes. `zlsim.py gate` rated the only primary series redundant: it is compression-equivalent and feature-close to local:grail_lgrs_kbr1c_ka_band_ranging_f64:kbr1c_range_accl_f64 (distance 0.0339 < 0.05, loss 0.0265 < 0.03). The next neighbours are GRAIL kbr1c_range_rate_f64 (0.0469), MACE equivariant weights (0.0589) and SXS strain modes (0.0593). The series' own ratio is 1.217: the values are full-mantissa noisy float64 (resampled 4 kHz → 2 kHz by the publisher), so their bytes resemble other noisy f64 families.
- Evidence: zlsim report (verdict WEAK, redundant=true, mode_share 0.0001). Gate PASS: values=56576000, bytes=452608000, samples=3536, median_values=16000. Verify OK, aggregate sha256 adef7d5f588e97b4d1365ec62285f8e810bf15727736d3bc94b22c2cc3f05c2d. Per-sample float32-exact count is about 0 and the ratio-test LCM is at least 1,061 bits. About 0.014% of values in 161 samples sit at the ±1000 µV input saturation (disclosed).
- Logs: .data/logs/dandi_000574_human_mtl_macro_ieeg_f64/{download,build,verify}.latest.log; .data/pipeline/logs/dandi_000574_human_mtl_macro_ieeg_f64/download.20261009_014959.log
- Decision: rejected as not novel at the byte level. The source, license, decoding and homogeneity were all sound. I did not re-lay out samples (for example channel-interleaved trial matrices) to dodge the metric, because that would tune the layout to the gate rather than collect a different material.
- Retry conditions: a reviewed policy decision that a channel-interleaved [16000, C] trial-epoch matrix (the dataset's native row-major layout) is an acceptable natural record, followed by a zlsim measurement that comes out OK or STRONG with that layout. Or a change in the zlsim library or thresholds under which this series is no longer equivalent to the GRAIL KBR1C family. The staged recipe (staging/dandi_000574_human_mtl_macro_ieeg_f64/) can be reused unchanged otherwise.
