# UK-DALE 2017 Individual-Appliance-Monitor (EcoManager plug) Real Power, Integer Watts at ~6 s, UInt16

Exclude these: - CT clamps (EcoManagerWholeHouseTx / CurrentCostTx channel_1 and the house_1 CT circuits), which record apparent power in VA up to 25 kW. - mains.dat (1 s SoundCardPowerMeter floats with voltage). - *_button_press.dat files.
