- Decode path: curl the pinned S3 keys. FITS: empty primary HDU, then a BINTABLE 'COMPRESSED_DATA' 1PB descriptors (count, heap offset) per row, ZCMPTYPE RICE_1, ZTILE 4144x1, BLOCKSIZE 32, BYTEPIX 2. Rice decoding per row: first 2 bytes are the seed pixel (big-endian int16). Each 32-pixel block has a 4-bit fs code: fs<0 means the block repeats; fs=14 means raw 16-bit differences; otherwise unary+fs-bit zigzag differences. Accumulate mod 2^16 and emit little-endian int16. Pure stdlib; a scratch decoder written during this probe decoded real rows correctly. Do not depend on CFITSIO: heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov is proxy-blocked.

- Risks: (1) Only ~1% of level-1 FUV files are full readouts (e.g. 8-23 per index chunk). Discovery must scan the index CSVs (iris_iris_data_iris_NNNN.csv, 1,640 files of ~1.2 MB) for fuv keys over ~2.4 MB, then confirm headers with 14 KB range reads. Index start/stop timestamps are corrupt (e.g. '0001-03-04'), so date from the s3key path. (2) Frames contain ~6% native BLANK -32768 fill (unread rows 0-~21 and the inter-CCD gap); document this as the missing-value policy and keep the frames whole. (3) The FUV continuum is faint, so much of each frame sits near the ~100-110 DN pedestal with emission lines on top. That is genuine but lower entropy than NUV, which is a possible sibling family. (4) The rights basis is the NASA SMD policy precedent; the page was not re-fetchable here. (5) Some full-readout LIGHT frames may belong to calibration programs; spread the selection across dates and OBSIDs (e.g. 16 frames, 2013-2016).
