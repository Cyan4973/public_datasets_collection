# NASA WMAP HEALPix Sky Maps Float32 — 2026-08-27

## Outcome

Accepted `nasa_wmap_healpix_sky_maps_f32`: seven separate full-sky field
families from five official NASA/LAMBDA WMAP nine-year frequency-band maps.

The recipe emits 35 natural samples containing 110,100,480 native float32
values and 440,401,920 primary bytes. Every sample contains the complete
3,145,728-pixel Nside-512 sphere in the source's NESTED HEALPix ordering.

## New numerical structure

This is the first accepted spherical-tessellation family. HEALPix maps are
one-dimensional equal-area sphere traversals whose index locality follows a
hierarchical sky partition, not rectangular row/column raster order. The
material therefore adds spatial structure unlike image planes, volumes,
catalog columns, time series, or Fourier-plane visibilities.

The seven homogeneous fields are temperature, Q polarization, U polarization,
effective observation count, and the QQ, QU, and UU polarization-weight-array
elements. Each field and frequency band is emitted separately; no physical
fields are interleaved.

## Source and rights

The K, Ka, Q, V, and W files are exact official WMAP DR5 products from NASA's
LAMBDA archive. Each 100,676,160-byte FITS object is pinned by URL, size, and
SHA-256, along with the official WMAP product page and current NASA guidance.

The pinned NASA page states that NASA is committed to open science and making
data available to everyone. Its AI section explicitly discusses NASA content
in AI development and says that use to train AI models does not constitute
NASA endorsement. The recipe emits only scientific numeric data: no logos,
insignia, people, page imagery, or third-party marked content. Source
disclosure must not imply NASA review, output attribution, permission, or
endorsement.

## Representation and duplicate handling

Every retained source column declares FITS `TFORM=E`, so all values are native
IEEE-754 float32. The build reverses each four-byte word from FITS big-endian
order to the identical little-endian representation without numerical
conversion, reprojection, reordering, smoothing, or masking.

Each file contains a `Stokes Maps` extension and a `Weight Arrays` extension.
Both publish `N_OBS`; the two copies are byte-identical in every band. The
recipe validates this invariant and emits one copy, avoiding 62,914,560 bytes
of exact duplication.

## Verification

All 110,100,480 retained values are finite, non-sentinel, and belong to
nonconstant, byte-distinct samples within their field family. Verification
pins the pages, complete three-HDU layouts, mission and band metadata, both
binary-table schemas, HEALPix geometry, source and output hashes, extrema,
distinctness probes, aggregate totals, index entries, filenames, and every
generated byte.
