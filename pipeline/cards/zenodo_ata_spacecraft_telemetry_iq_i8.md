- Homogeneity: One acquisition and publication chain: ATA 6 m dish, RFCB LO d at 512 MHz IF, USRP N321/N320, GNU Radio 3.10, downsampled and converted to ci8 by the same author. Every file is a deep-space telemetry downlink, mostly X-band (Orion is S-band). Sample rate varies by recording (256 ksps ESCAPADE, 2.048 Msps JUICE/Euclid/Hera, 4.096 Msps Psyche, 10.24 Msps Orion), and so does the per-recording 8-bit scaling: 1 MiB mid-file probes gave sd 5.2 (JUICE), 14.8 (Orion), 19.4 (ESCAPADE), 23.0 (Hera) and 33.6 (Psyche), all zero-mean with no clipping. If the judge wants a stricter regime, restrict to the six 2.048 Msps X-band files (JUICE 1a/5c, Euclid p1/p2, Hera x/y). Excluded: same-author files declared ci16_le or cf32_le (Psyche decimated, Hera part 1, LunaH-Map, Voyager-1, Chang'e 5, etc.).
- Risks: (1) Only 17 natural records, inside the ~20 'acceptable when limited' guidance. Each is one window of an oversized recording, so the builder must justify the window as a rule-7 bounded subset. (2) Near-Gaussian int8 noise with a buried carrier may sit close to other int8 noise-like families: the GNSS candidate below, SHARAD EDR i8 (downloaded, pending) and ASCAD i8 traces. The wide sd spread should help, but that is unmeasured. (3) The int8 values are a post-hoc 8-bit conversion of 16-bit USRP samples by the author. That conversion is the published artifact, so it is native to the source, but say so in the manifest. (4) Per-file md5 covers the whole file only, so pin window SHA-256 instead.
