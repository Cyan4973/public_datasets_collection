- Decode path: Pure stdlib TIFF/COG reader: parse IFD 0 (full resolution) with struct (width, height, TileWidth/TileLength 256, TileOffsets 324, TileByteCounts 325, Compression 8 = Adobe Deflate, Predictor 1 = none, SampleFormat 1, GDAL_NODATA '0').
