# Aloft UVA_VPTS German Weather-Radar Vertical Profiles of Animal Reflectivity (eta) Float32

- Candidate id: `aloft_uva_vpts_animal_reflectivity_f32`
- Width: float32
- Quantity: eta: reflectivity of biological scatterers (mostly migrating birds), in cm2/km3. vol2bird computes it from DWD C-band weather-radar volumes in 25 altitude bins of 200 m (0-4800 m), about every 5 minutes. This is the core radar-aeroecology quantity that bird migration density is derived from.

- Natural record: One radar-month VPTS file, which is the deposit's documented file unit (country/radar/year/month .csv.gz). It is emitted as the time-by-height eta matrix in stored row order (datetime, then height). A single 5-minute profile has only 25 values per variable and is one time step of the series, not an independent record.

- Decode path: curl the 465 uva/monthly/de*/YYYY/*_vpts_YYYYMM.csv.gz objects (pin size and ETag from the S3 listing). Read them with gzip plus stdlib csv.DictReader and take the eta column. Map an empty cell or 'NaN' to the canonical float32 NaN and count it as missing. Otherwise convert float(text) with struct.pack('<f'). The CSV text is the shortest double repr of the stored float32 (e.g. 56.06618118286133), so the conversion is exact. Validate the header against vpts-csv-table-schema.json and require 25 heights from 0 to 4800 m.
- Novelty kind: new_modality
- Novelty evidence: novelty.py with the S3 and Zenodo URLs and the terms aloft, vpts, 'vertical profile', bird and migration found no URL matches beyond the generic same-host zenodo.org. There are no recipe, registry, ledger or downstream hits for aloft, vpts or vertical-profile terms; the only 'bird' hit is met_biocollection_objects, a museum catalog. The radar families in the corpus are 8-bit raw reflectivity or velocity rasters (NEXRAD NIDS, FMI PPI, SEVIR VIL). None is a biological-scatterer vertical profile time series, and there is no radar-aeroecology family at any width.
- Homogeneity: 18 DWD C-band radars processed by one UvA vol2bird chain. coverage.csv shows unique_heights=25 for every UVA radar, so the height lattice is identical, and the unit is the same throughout. One variable only. Excluded as redundant or different quantities: dens (= eta/rcs with rcs fixed at 11 cm2; checked 56.06618/11 = 5.0969257), dbz (a log transform of eta), dbz_all (includes precipitation), and u/v/w/ff/dd/sd_vvp (kinematics). The Belgian (100 radar-months, 1.26 GB) and Dutch (327 radar-months, 4.38 GB) UVA radars are also homogeneous C-band data, but are left out to keep the download bounded. Germany+Belgium (565 files, about 2.5 GB, about 194 MB primary) is a fallback within the 5 GB cap.
- Risks: (1) Rule 8: the judge may argue that 5-minute profiles are the natural unit. The defence is that the VPTS time series is the published deposit unit and bioRad's documented 'vpts' object, and that one profile (25 values) is a time step, not an independent record. (2) About 5-7% of eta cells are missing (blank), and the format uses blank in uva files but 'NaN' in baltrad files; handle both. (3) Partial months: about 61k values per radar-month on average. (4) Rights context: Aloft's baltrad prefix depends on an OPERA 'research-only license agreement' for its raw inputs, so do NOT use baltrad/. The uva/ deposit (RMI, DWD, KNMI inputs) is CC0 on Zenodo and in the bucket FAQ, and the German subset comes from DWD open data. (5) Bucket objects can be regenerated, so pin sizes and ETags; Zenodo de.tgz (1,231,349,011 bytes) is a frozen alternative.
