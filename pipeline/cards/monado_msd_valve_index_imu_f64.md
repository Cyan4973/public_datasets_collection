- Homogeneity: One device (Valve Index), one IMU, one ~1000 Hz device clock, one post-processing pipeline (Basalt IMU calibration applied to every sequence). Gyro (rad/s) and accel (m/s^2) are kept as two separate primary series so units never mix within a sample; this follows the TUM translation+quaternion precedent. The HP Reverb G2 (42 usable) and Samsung Odyssey+ (28 usable) sequences use the same format and units but different IMUs. They are deliberately excluded to avoid a mixed-sensor objection and could form a later family. Use data.csv (corrected), not data.raw.csv (10-decimal raw) or data.extra.csv (host arrival times).
- Risks: (1) The long-session archives are corrupt or truncated on HF: the MIPB08 central directory describes ~66 GB of members inside an 11.45 GB object, and local-header offsets fall past EOF (HTTP 416). MGO12 has the same problem (sum of csz is 3.44× the object size), and MOO12 likely does too. The recipe must verify the local signature and CRC and exclude failures (MIPB08 for the Valve Index scope). (2) Corrected values are a pinned upstream calibration (M·raw − b) rather than raw counts. They are the dataset's canonical EuRoC-format IMU stream, but a judge could ask why not data.raw.csv. (3) Text-to-binary ratio is ~3:1, and downloading CDs for the longest playing sessions adds ~10-30 MB each. (4) Per-sequence durations vary widely; MIO09-11 are only seconds long but still exceed the 1,000-value median floor.
