# EHT Public UVFITS Visibilities Float32 — 2026-08-27

## Outcome

Accepted `eht_public_uvfits_visibilities_f32`: separate real, imaginary, and
statistical-weight sequences from five calibrated Event Horizon Telescope
UVFITS observations spanning M87 (2017 and 2018), 3C 279, Centaurus A, and
Sagittarius A*.

The recipe contributes 176,439 native float32 values and 705,756 primary
bytes. It emits fifteen samples: one sample per observation and numeric field.
Natural sample lengths range from 8,004 to 17,529 values, with a median of
9,744.

## New domain and structure

This is the first accepted calibrated radio-interferometric visibility family.
Unlike image planes, detector events, light curves, or single-instrument raw
I/Q, these values are sparse Fourier-plane measurements made across telescope
baselines and observation time. The real and imaginary components form aligned
complex visibilities, while the separate positive weight series describes
measurement confidence.

The three physical fields are emitted as distinct homogeneous streams. They
are not interleaved into record tuples.

## Source and license

All five observations come from official repositories in the
`eventhorizontelescope` GitHub organization and are pinned to exact commits,
paths, sizes, Git blob SHA-1s, and SHA-256s. Each release carries an identical
data-specific notice stating that the data files are licensed under the Open
Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License 1.0. The GPLv3 portion of the
notice applies to release scripts, not these data payloads.

## UVFITS filtering and endian handling

The selected files use FITS random groups with native big-endian IEEE-754
float32 `(real, imaginary, weight)` triplets and four declared polarization
slots: RR, LL, RL, and LR.

Every selected Stokes-I observation stores RR and LL as byte-identical copies
for all three fields. The recipe validates this invariant and emits one copy
per random group, avoiding an artificial twofold duplication. RL and LR are
accepted only as structural placeholders: their real and imaginary values
must be zero and their weights must be zero or positive infinity. They are not
training material.

Each retained four-byte FITS word is reversed directly into little-endian
order. This preserves the exact IEEE-754 bit pattern without arithmetic
conversion, normalization, gridding, imaging, or magnitude/phase conversion.

## Verification

The dependency-free verifier reparses complete FITS HDU layouts and pins the
license notices, source objects and dates, random-group dimensions and
parameters, Stokes coding, retained and rejected counts, source and output
hashes, extrema, distinct-word counts, aggregate totals, index rows, filenames,
and every generated byte. All retained values are finite; all weights are
strictly positive; all fifteen samples are nonconstant and byte-distinct within
their series.
