AIFF (FORM/AIFF): parse the COMM chunk (channels i16, frames u32, bits i16, 80-bit extended rate; assert 10 ch, 16 bit, 2000 Hz) and the SSND chunk (offset u32, blocksize u32, then interleaved big-endian int16 frames). Deinterleave with struct.unpack('>10h') or array('h') plus byteswap. Do not use the aifc module (removed in 3.13); the header is trivial to parse by hand.

- Risks: (1) License: no explicit CC/PD tag in the ISO record. Rights rest on U.S. Government authorship plus the NODD 'used as desired' statement (same basis as the accepted noaa_wcsd recipe). (2) 12-bit data in 16-bit (low nibble always 0, like the accepted TUM VI card), with low variance (sd about 30-130 counts around a DC offset of about -500 to -1000), so it is quite compressible. (3) Dead channels are entirely zero and must be dropped; the first file of a deployment (SBNMS_20071218_133000) was mostly zeros, so the builder must check for non-zero variance. (4) Each deployment has >1000 files, so pick pinned names with sizes.
