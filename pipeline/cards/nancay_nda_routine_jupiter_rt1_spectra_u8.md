# Nançay Decameter Array (NDA) Routine Receiver Jupiter Observations: Native 8-bit Swept-Frequency Log-Power Dynamic Spectra (RT1 raw, 400 channels, LH/RH), UInt8

- Candidate id: `nancay_nda_routine_jupiter_rt1_spectra_u8`
- Width: uint8
- Quantity: Detected decametric radio power (log-compressed 8-bit receiver output) per frequency channel of the NDA Routine swept-frequency analyzer (400 channels, about 10-40 MHz). Sweeps alternate between the left-hand and right-hand circularly polarised halves of the 144-antenna phased array, at about 0.5 s cadence.

Recommended sample: one polarization stream of one file (records of that polarization, 400 values each, in time order). That gives 2 samples per file, a channel-level split of the file rather than a sharding of it.

- Risks: (1) Breadth risk vs noaa_rstn_sagamore_hill_srs_spectra_u8. Both are 8-bit log-power swept spectra with about 400 channels. Rough probe on 3,600 bytes: NDA H0=6.08, H1=3.69, delta-H=4.56, mean 103; RSTN band A H0=6.35, H1=3.08, delta-H=4.16, mean 49. That is similar but not identical, so zlsim could rate it WEAK.
