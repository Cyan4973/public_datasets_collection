- Width: int16
- Quantity: Raw tri-axial acceleration counts (X, Y, Z) from a Bosch CISS sensor mounted inside brownfield CNC milling machines at 2 kHz, one recording per executed machining operation (15 processes OP00-OP14, 3 machines, 6 half-year timeframes 2019-2021, labelled good/bad). - Risks: (1) Width argument: source containers store the counts as float32 or float64.
